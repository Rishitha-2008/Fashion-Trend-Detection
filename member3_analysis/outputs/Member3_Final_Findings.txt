4. CONCLUSION

Black and solid-pattern clothing are prominent in both datasets.
Clothing category distributions differ between the image sample
and H&M sales data.

The image dataset contains 150 samples, so its findings should
be treated as exploratory rather than representative of the
entire fashion market.

The comparison percentages are calculated among matched labels
in the two datasets.
